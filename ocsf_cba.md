# OCSF Project Cost Benefit Analysis using Trieve

## OVERVIEW

Our business objective is to map 15 out of 35 log sources to the Open Cybersecurity Schema Framework (OCSF) by the end of May (3 months). **Missing this deadline carries the risk of losing the opportunity to unify security reporting with OCSF of there being ongoing labor-intensive efforts which provide less accurate and consistent insights.**

Trieve, having successfully collaborated during the proof-of-concept stage, will offer hands-on expertise by deploying and fine-tuning the RAG-powered VRL mappings to dramatically increase the odds of success.

## COST ANALYSIS

### With Trieve

- Trieve’s service fee: $3,000 per month
- Total for 3 months: $3,000 × 3 = $9,000

### Without Trieve

- Opportunity cost of building, deploying, and maintaining vector embedding inference infrastructure 
- Manpower (likely 2 people) to deploy and manage a vector DB (est. 2+ weeks)
- Manpower (likely 2 people) to build dense vector linear combination pipeline for clustering (est. 2+ weeks)
- Opportunity cost of building the above without best practices and custom-fitting informed expertise by massive-scale experiences

### BENEFIT ANALYSIS

- If internal efforts to build these mappings manually would require significant additional manpower (potentially costing many times $9,000 over 3 months) and risk project delays, then Trieve’s $9,000 investment represents huge long term cost savings in both direct expenditure and risk avoidance.

- The opportunity to standardize across the organization can result in further operational efficiencies and lower support/maintenance costs over time.

## RISK/OPPORTUNITY COST

### Without Trieve’s accelerated and expert approach, we risk:

1. Deploying requisite infrastructure could take up time that could be better spent working on the actual deliverable of the project
2. Deployed Infrastructure doesn't scale to the required throughput for a full-scale deployment at end of project
3. Missing the deadline and decommissioning critical data sources (e.g., Google Chronicle).
4. Continuing to commit continuous and unpredictable manpower resources to build/maintain bespoke solutions.
5. Delaying the strategic transition to standardized security reporting (OCSF) with long-term implications on operational agility and compliance.

### With Trieve's assistance and solution:

1. Guaranteed ability to deploy requisite infrastructure within a week
2. Confidence that infrastructure, especially regarding vector inference, will scale up to the needs of a fully delivered solution
3. There is a total cost of $9,000; however, the avoidance of risks and reduction in internal development overhead makes it a highly favorable proposition.
4. The solution also positions the enterprise to potentially leverage this standardized framework for additional integrations in the future (such as Olly documentation RAG bot), compounding the value.

## CONCLUSION

The total cost of $9,000 for 3 months of Trieve’s RAG/VRL expertise should be viewed within the context of:

- Improving speed of deploying requisite infrastructure and confidence in running it.
- Avoiding critical decommissioning of key sources that could derail security reporting.
- Eliminating the heavy, ongoing internal labor costs associated with bespoke regex solutions.
- Enabling a timely strategic move to an industry-standard approach (OCSF) that increases operational efficiency, compliance, and future scalability.

Trieve’s solution is a cost-effective and strategically sound option that significantly improves the trajectory of our project's success and positions us for enhanced security reporting capabilities in the long run.
